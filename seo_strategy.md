# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages for Sydney Occupational Services
- Public service, team, location, referral, and contact routes

## Out of scope
- Authentication and internal application infrastructure
- Development-only preview and install tooling except where it changes public page metadata

## Target audience
- NSW employers, insurers, case managers, rehabilitation stakeholders, and people seeking occupational rehabilitation services

## Primary topics
- Workplace rehabilitation in Sydney and NSW
- Occupational rehabilitation, workplace assessments, ergonomic assessments, capacity evaluations, vocational assessments, and return-to-work services

## Crawler assumptions
- Public routes are server-rendered with TanStack Start and should be indexable and shareable.
- Search, social-preview, and AI crawlers should receive meaningful metadata and primary content in initial HTML.

## Dismissed categories
- None.
